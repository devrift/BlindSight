1 · System power map

🔴 Rule 1 — The Pi's ground must never touch the buck's ground. All Pi traffic is wireless (Wi-Fi HTTP), so no common reference is needed.
✅ Rule 2 — ESP32, CAM, DAC and sensors must share one common ground. They exchange signals, so they need a common reference.

3 · The ECHO divider (build 3)

⚠️ GPIO 34/35/39 are input-only with no internal protection. Wiring a raw 5V ECHO straight to them damages the pin. Build all three dividers on a scrap perfboard — not on alligator clips.

4 · ESP32-CAM ← real USB-TTL (TX/RX are CROSSED)

🔴 Biggest new gotcha: With the Arduino-as-TTL trick the labels lined up straight (D0→U0R). With a real USB-TTL they cross: TTL TXD → CAM U0R and CAM U0T → TTL RXD. Get this backwards and you get "Failed to connect."
✅ Set the adapter jumper to 3V3 → the 1k/2k divider on U0R is no longer needed. That's the main reason a real TTL beats the Arduino hack. If your adapter is 5V-only, keep the divider on that one line.
⚠️ Power the CAM from the buck, not the TTL. Adapters supply 100–500 mA; the CAM peaks near 800 mA. Keep the TTL for data + GND only — but the GND wire is still required as the signal reference.

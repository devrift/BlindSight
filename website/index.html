ENGINEERING BLUEPRINT

The Three-Tier Safety Hierarchy

Critical safety lives at the silicon level. Cognitive intelligence lives in the companion.

TIER 0 • INSTINCTIVE REFLEX Autonomous

Smart Glasses Hardware Arc

Directly executes on the head-mounted ESP32. Operates without Wi-Fi, bluetooth, or cellular service. Employs 3 HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors firing pulses to command instantaneous evasive actions through I2S audio clips.

  • < 25 cm Distance: Critical STOP override (stop.wav)
  • 25 – 45 cm Distance: Directional steering away from closer hazard (left.wav / right.wav)
  • 45 – 100 cm: Cautionary warning (careful.wav)
  • > 100 cm: Clear path confirmation (clear.wav)
  • ESP-NOW Radio: Wire-free frame link from camera module
TIER 1 • EDGE COMPUTER VISION Offline Machine Vision

Local Machine Vision Engine

A sandboxed YOLOv8-nano model running in ONNX Runtime at ~1.5s per frame on edge hardware. Identifies key obstacles that sonar cannot grasp: staircases, curbs, glass doors, and walking corridors.

  • 80 Pretrained Classes: People, vehicles, chairs, doors, stairs
  • Subprocess Isolation: Inference faults never crash safety audio
  • Disk Ring Buffer: Automatic 50MB quota sweeping prevents storage leaks
  • Fail-Closed API: Serves real detections or explicit fallback alerts
TIER 2 • COGNITIVE COMPANION Voice-First AI

Multimodal AI Companion

The intelligent conversation layer running on the user's phone or browser. Responds to natural questions, maintains personal memory facts, and delivers natural neural speech.

  • Wake Word Matching: "Hey BlindSight" with continuous listen & silence segmentation
  • Persistent Memory Bank: "Remember my keys are on the desk" (stored offline)
  • 7 Reply Languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Spanish, French
  • Zero Hallucination: Never fabricates a scene if vision AI is offline

ETHICAL AI FOR ACCESSIBILITY

The Fail-Closed Honesty Contract

Why assistive mobility AI must never guess or fabricate observations.

❌

Optimistic AI (Dangerous Fallback)

Traditional AI assistants invent plausible answers when offline or uncertain:

  • Hallucinates that "the hallway is completely empty" when camera is disconnected.
  • Pretends to see objects that were present in a previous frame minutes ago.
  • Silent failures when network disconnects mid-sentence.
Result: High Collision & Injury Hazard
🛡️

BlindSight Honesty Contract (Fail-Closed)

BlindSight is hard-coded to never fabricate a scene description:

  • Explicit spoken disclosure: "Image captured, but vision AI is not connected. I will not guess your surroundings."
  • Tier 0 ultrasonic stop alerts always supersede AI dialogue.
  • Spoken network diagnostics and 2-second automatic retries.
Result: 100% Reliable User Safety
